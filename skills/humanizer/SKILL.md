# Humanizer

Write and edit prose so it reads like a human technical writer: direct, specific, uninflated. Preserve all factual claims; cut all decorative weight.

## Structural Tells (Banned Patterns)

1. **No "Not X, but Y":** Never build artificial contrast (*"Not just a tool, but a revolution"*). State the fact directly.
2. **No Staged Run-ups or Closers:** Cut openers (*"Let's dive in"*, *"Here's what you need to know"*, *"Honestly"*) and dramatic one-line closers (*"That is the real win"*, *"Let that sink in"*).
3. **No Aphorisms or Pseudo-depth:** Cut abstract analogies (*"X is the currency of Y"*, *"at its core"*, *"fundamentally"*).
4. **No Forced Triads:** Do not force groupings of three (*"speed, scale, and simplicity"*) unless logically necessary.
5. **No Decorative Formatting:** Never use bold labels on every list item (`- **Feature:** text`), emojis in headers, or excessive em-dashes (—). Use plain sentences, commas, or periods.

## Vocabulary Blacklist

Never use AI stock jargon unless quoting or referring to an exact technical term:
* *Verbs/Adverbs:* delve, foster, bolster, underscore, highlight, meticulously, quietly.
* *Nouns:* tapestry, landscape (abstract), testament, interplay, intricacies.
* *Adjectives:* crucial, pivotal, robust (figurative), vibrant, groundbreaking, multifaceted.
* *Sales fluff:* nestled, boasting, unparalleled, exciting times ahead.

## Output Standard

- **Direct Facts:** Replace *"serves as a testament to"* with *"is"*, and *"features a diverse array of"* with *"has"*.
- **Neutral Tone:** State limits and bugs plainly without chatbot apologies (*"I hope this helps"*), disclaimers (*"as of my knowledge cutoff"*), or defensive hedging.
- **Rhythm:** Alternate sentence lengths naturally.
